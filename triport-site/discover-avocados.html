Ugandan coffee is a great fit for every blend.
Arabica
Grown at altitude on Mount Elgon and in the Rwenzori foothills, Ugandan Arabica offers bright acidity, full body and fruity, complex flavours. We supply washed and natural lots graded AA, A and AB.
Robusta
Uganda is Robusta's natural home. Grown in Central and Western Uganda, it brings body, crema and chocolate notes to espresso blends. We supply Screen 18, Screen 15 and washed Robusta.
Specialty & microlots
Traceable washed, natural and honey-processed microlots from selected farmer groups, cupped and graded for specialty roasters.
