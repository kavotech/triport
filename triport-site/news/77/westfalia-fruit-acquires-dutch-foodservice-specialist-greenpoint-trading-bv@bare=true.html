Supplying Europe's foodservice market with Ugandan produce
Europe's foodservice market buys Ugandan coffee, fresh produce and grains for restaurants, hotels and caterers.
Foodservice buyers need consistent specifications, reliable volumes and clear documentation for every delivery.

We supply export-grade avocados, pineapples, mangoes and vegetables, as well as Arabica and Robusta coffee and cleaned grains.

Fresh produce can fly from Entebbe for fast delivery, while coffee and grains ship by container through Mombasa.

We prepare phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin and the compliance paperwork European markets require.

 

Our team agrees grades, pack sizes and delivery windows in writing before every order.


“Foodservice buyers value suppliers who deliver what they promise, every time,” our sales team explains.
