Triport Agro International Limited Ethical Trading Statement

(Applies to our operations and supply chains in Uganda)

 

INTRODUCTION

This statement sets out how Triport Agro International Limited works to ensure fair and ethical trade across its operations and supply chains.

Triport Agro International Limited is incorporated in Uganda and exports agricultural products to international markets.

This statement is our commitment to ensuring that forced labour, child labour and human trafficking do not occur in our operations or supply chains.

 

ABOUT TRIPORT AGRO INTERNATIONAL LIMITED

Triport Agro International Limited is a Kampala-based exporter of Ugandan coffee, fresh fruits, vegetables and grains, supplying retail, foodservice and wholesale customers in Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

 

COMMITMENT

Our management team accepts responsibility for preventing labour exploitation in our own operations and in the supply chains we buy from.

 

SUPPLY CHAINS

We build long-term relationships with organised farmer groups and cooperatives across Uganda, and we know the regions and groups behind the produce we export.

 

OUR APPROACH

We are committed to transparency in how we source and trade. Our approach combines clear policies, direct relationships with farmers and regular contact with the groups we buy from.

 

POLICIES

Our policies reflect our commitment to ethical business, including fair pricing, honest contracts and zero tolerance of forced or child labour.

 

ASSESSING AND MANAGING RISK

We consider the risks in each sourcing region and product, and we focus our attention where the risks are highest.

Our sourcing team visits farmer groups and discusses working conditions as part of our regular relationship with them.

 

DUE DILIGENCE AND REMEDIATION

Where buyers require third-party audits or certifications, we work with farmer groups that hold them or help arrange the documentation needed.

We know that issues can arise even in well-managed supply chains, so we keep in close contact with the groups we source from.

If a concern is raised, we investigate it on a case-by-case basis and work with the people involved on a fair remedy.

 

INTERNAL & EXTERNAL STAKEHOLDERS

We know that labour exploitation cannot be addressed by any single business, and we are open to working with buyers and partners on shared solutions.

We share information about our sourcing with buyers on request to support their own due diligence.

We discuss ethical trading expectations with our commercial partners as part of our ongoing relationships.

Responsibility for ethical trading is shared across our sales, operations and legal functions.

 

TRAINING

Our team is briefed on our ethical trading commitments and knows how to recognise and report concerns.

 

SUBJECT MATTER EXPERTISE

Our Legal Administrator oversees contracts, compliance and governance, including how concerns are handled.

 

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

We monitor how our sourcing relationships work in practice and review our approach as we grow.

We believe that openness is the best way to find and fix problems, and we encourage anyone with a concern to speak up.

We commit to addressing labour risks, engaging with farmers and workers and cooperating with the relevant authorities.

Concerns can be reported to us in confidence through our contact page.

 

FUTURE FOCUS & CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

We will continue to strengthen our relationships with the farmer groups we source from.

We will keep reviewing our policies and practices as Triport Agro grows.

This statement has been approved by the management of Triport Agro International Limited.

Triport Agro International Limited is Uganda's trusted agricultural export partner.
