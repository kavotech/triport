Our Products
Quality products, season after season.

Triport Agro is a trusted supplier of Ugandan coffee, fresh produce and grains to international markets.

Coffee
Specialty and commercial Arabica and Robusta from Uganda's high-altitude regions. Explore all of our coffee.
Fresh fruits & vegetables
Explore our export-grade fruits and vegetables such as avocados, pineapples, mangoes and hot peppers.
Grains & legumes
Discover our cleaned and sorted staples including sesame, beans, maize and spices.
We deliver world-class Ugandan produce to every market we serve.
Consistent supply
We source from trusted partner farmers across Uganda's growing seasons and plan volumes with buyers, so supply stays consistent.
Strict quality grading
Every lot is selected, graded and packed to meet destination-market standards for freshness, consistency and shelf life.
Export documentation support
Our aim is to make importing easy. We handle certification, phytosanitary documents and shipment coordination for every order.
